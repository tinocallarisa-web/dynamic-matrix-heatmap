WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.0.1.0
Mostly correctness in how values are combined: a counted field returned the number of row
groups, a distinct count bound as a measure was added up, and averages can now be weighted
through a new optional "Weights for averages" well. Numbers use cultureSelector, so separators
follow the report locale. Purchase path: clearLicenseNotification, then notifyFeatureBlocked
naming the feature, then notifyLicenseRequired ~10 s later, since Power BI shows one
notification at a time and the last replaces the previous; the timer is cancelled in
destroy(). Bookmarks: selection rebuilt from options.jsonFilters.
hostCapabilities.allowInteractions honoured. stringResources en-US and es-ES. No new
dependency, no new privilege.

PRO PREVIEW (watermark, per the guidelines)
Free user, edit mode, licence resolved, supported environment: Pro capabilities render under
a "Pro preview" watermark naming them. Reading view, before the licence resolves, or where it
cannot be read: free result, no watermark, nothing persisted.

PRIVACY
No network calls, no telemetry, no storage. Only the dataView; the picked dimensions and
value are persisted in the .pbix. privileges is []. The TupleFilter $schema is an identifier,
not a requested URL. No innerHTML anywhere.

FREE VS PRO
Free: X/Y/Value dropdowns, heatmap, formatting, tooltips, cross-filter, context menu,
keyboard, high contrast, weighted averages, 3 dimensions, 2 measures.
Pro: 10 dimensions, 5 measures, Distinct count of, Show value as %, totals.

watermark. Click a cell: other visuals filter. Bookmark it, leave the page and return: filter
and highlight come back. Right click: context menu. Tab + Enter: same as click.
