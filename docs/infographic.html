Tips

  • Put the high-cardinality dimension on Y — rows scroll, columns do not
  • Use diagonal headers for long column labels
  • Three or four dimensions beat ten, for speed and usability
  • One outlier flattens the whole colour scale — filter it out
  • Use Dash when empty means something other than zero
  • Cross-filter to a related table needs direction Both
