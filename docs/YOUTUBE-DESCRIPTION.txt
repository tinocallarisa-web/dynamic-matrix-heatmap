--- DESCRIPTION ---

A Power BI matrix answers the question its author had. Readers who want the rows by product instead of region, or the value as a share of the total, have to ask for another matrix.

Dynamic Matrix Heatmap is a pivot table with a heatmap that the reader turns. Rows, columns, the value and how it is shown are chosen in a panel inside the visual, and the heatmap redraws. Nobody edits the report, and there is no DAX.

WHAT IT DOES
• Rows, columns and the value chosen by whoever reads the report
• Show as absolute, % of row, % of column or % of grand total, per measure (Pro)
• Row groups with subtotals that open and close, coloured on their own scale (Pro)
• Totals in one click (Pro)
• Click a column header to sort; days of the week and months in calendar order
• Others: the smallest categories are grouped so the totals still add up
• Order rows and columns by similarity (Pro)
• Exact numbers: an ID in Detail (rows) makes averages exact; Count (Distinct) is counted exactly
• 12 colour schemes, colour rules and 5 style templates
• Cross-filtering by cell, group or subtotal; bookmarks; format on the canvas
• Side panel, top bar or hidden controls; 12 languages; keyboard and high contrast
• No network access: nothing leaves your report

FREE AND PRO
Free is a correct pivot with 3 dimensions and 2 measures, the heatmap, rules, templates, Others, sorting and exact averages. Pro raises the limits to 10 dimensions and 5 measures and adds Show as, totals, row groups, order by similarity and distinct counts of Detail fields. While editing without a licence, Pro features are shown working under a watermark.
